    What this measures: playback only models the pronunciation; it cannot hear you. “Check what you said” uses speech recognition, which reports whether your words were understood, not how native your accent sounds. Recognition can accept a slightly wrong sound or reject a correct one, so treat the score as a guide. In Chrome and Edge, speech recognition sends your audio to the browser maker’s servers; nothing is sent until you press Record.

    Browser support on this device

      Voices come from your operating system, so the choice and quality differ between devices. Word highlighting depends on the voice: many online voices do not report word positions, and then only the sentence is highlighted.

      Text recognition: Tesseract.js with tessdata_fast models. Speech: your browser’s Web Speech API. English IPA: CMU Pronouncing Dictionary.
